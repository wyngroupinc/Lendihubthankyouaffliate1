Before You Decide

Questions you probably have

We'd rather answer them here than hide them in fine print.

Will this hurt my credit score?

Checking your options through Lendihub does not involve a credit inquiry from us. Some lenders may use a soft pull to show you a personalized rate — soft pulls don't affect your score. A hard inquiry only happens if you formally apply with a specific lender and authorize it.

What's the catch? How is this free?

Lenders pay us a referral fee when a match moves forward. We only make money when you find a deal good enough to accept. If you walk away, we earn zero — so our whole model depends on showing you real options.

My credit isn't great. Should I even bother?

Yes. Many of our lending partners specialize in fair and rebuilding credit. They weigh income, employment, and stability — not just your score. You might be surprised what's available.

How much can I borrow?

Lenders in our network offer personal loans ranging from roughly $1,000 to $50,000. The amount you qualify for depends on your income, credit profile, and the individual lender's criteria.

Am I committing to anything?

No. 100% non-binding. See what's available, and if nothing fits — you're done. No contracts, no hidden terms, no obligation to accept any offer.

How fast can I get the money?

It varies by lender, but many can fund as fast as the next business day after approval and verification. Your specific timeline depends on the lender you choose and your bank's processing times.
