The Real Reason

They did not turn down your business. They turned down a number.

Here is what happens at a bank. Your file goes into a scoring system. It looks at your personal credit score, how long you have been open, and your industry code. Out comes a number. A person barely reads it.

That number does not know you just had your best quarter. It does not know your biggest customer always pays, just slowly. It does not know that $40,000 a month moves through your account like clockwork.

You did not fail their test. They ran the wrong one. A credit score grades you as a person, mostly on things from years ago. It was never built to measure a business that is running fine today.

So why has nobody told you about the second one?

Your bank has no reason to send you somewhere else. And the lenders who do read deposits mostly advertise big numbers instead of explaining how they decide anything.

The second way is not a secret. It just never gets explained. So here it is.

The First Way And The Second

One reads your credit file. The other reads your bank account.

Same business. Same numbers. Two completely different tests, and almost nobody explains the difference to you.

The first way: credit-score underwriting
  • Starts with your personal FICO score
  • Weighs things that happened years ago
  • Runs a hard credit pull just to look
  • Treats your industry code as a risk flag
  • Wants 2 years of tax returns and filings
  • Takes weeks, and often ends in a no with no reason given
The second way: deposit-based underwriting
  • Starts with money moving through your business account
  • Weighs the last 3 to 6 months, not the last 6 years
  • No credit pull to show you options
  • Cares how steady the money is, not what you sell
  • Reads bank statements you already have
  • Moves in days, and tells you what is missing

Neither test is magic. One just asks a question your business can actually answer.

What They Actually Read

The second way is three things.

That is it. Once you know the three, you can tell in about a minute whether you are close.

1. How much comes in

Total deposits each month. This is the biggest single thing they look at. Most partners want to see at least $10,000 a month.

2. How steady it is

Days your account went negative. Payments that bounced. Steady beats big. A smaller business with clean statements often reads better than a bigger one with a messy account.

3. What is already coming out

Advances you are still paying back. This is the one that trips most people up. Two or more running at once and the answer is usually no.

Before You Decide

Questions you probably have

We would rather answer them here than bury them in fine print.

Do I have to send bank statements right now?

No. The questions here take about a minute and ask for rough numbers. If a partner wants to make you an offer, that is when statements come up. Nothing is needed up front.

Will this hurt my credit?

No. We do not pull your credit at all. Not a hard pull, not a soft pull. If you later apply with a lender, that is their call and they have to tell you first.

What is the catch? Why is this free?

Lending partners pay us when they fund a business. If you do not take an offer, we do not get paid. There is no fee to you, now or later.

Will money start coming out of my account?

Not from us. We never touch your account. If you sign with a lender, they set the payment and the schedule. Get the amount, how often, and the total payback in writing before you sign.

My score is bad. Does the deposit thing really work?

There is no minimum score to check. Deposits get read first. Your score can still matter to a lender at the end, but it is not what stops the file from being opened.

I already have an advance. Can I still get funded?

Maybe, but it is harder. Most partners want the one you have mostly paid down before they add another. Say so up front and we will be straight with you instead of wasting your week.

Am I going to get 20 phone calls?

You will hear from lending partners who have something real to offer. We do not sell your details to a call list.

How fast does this move?

Checking takes about a minute. After that it depends on the lender and how fast you send what they ask for. Some move same day. Nobody can promise you a date, and be careful with anyone who does.
