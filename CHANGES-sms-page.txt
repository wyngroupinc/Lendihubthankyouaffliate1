A BUG I CAUGHT AND FIXED
------------------------
My first attempt put the disclosure INSIDE <div id="leadcapture-form">,
which is the div the LeadCapture embed writes the form into and that
your MutationObserver watches. When the real form loaded it would very
likely have wiped the disclosure, so it would have looked fine in a
screenshot and been missing in production.

It is now a sibling <section> AFTER the form section closes. Verified by
simulating the embed overwriting that container: the disclosure survives.
