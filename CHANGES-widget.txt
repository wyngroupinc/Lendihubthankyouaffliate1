TWO THINGS TO LOOK AT
---------------------
1. PRIVACY POLICY. The widget loads third-party JavaScript on every page
   and collects whatever a visitor types into chat, including name and
   phone number. privacy.html names the third parties who receive data;
   LeadConnector / GoHighLevel is not among them. Worth adding before
   this goes live, for the same reason the session-recording disclosure
   was added on 8 Oct.

2. form.html. The chat bubble floats bottom-right, which is where the
   funnel's submit button and consent checkbox sit on mobile. If it
   overlaps them it will cost conversions, and the consent block needs to
   stay visible for TrustedForm. Load lendihub.com/form on a phone and
   look before leaving the widget on that page. Removing it from that one
   file is a one-line deletion.
