Before You Decide

Questions you probably have

Are you the lender?

No. We are the matching step. We do not approve anything, set any rate, or hold your money. Lending partners review your file and make their own decisions.

Will this hurt my credit?

No. We do not pull your credit at all. If you later apply with a lender, that is their call and they have to tell you first.

What is the catch? Why is this free?

Lending partners pay us when they fund a business. If you do not take an offer, we do not get paid. There is no fee to you.

Will money start coming out of my account?

Not from us. We never touch your account. If you sign with a lender, they set the payment and the schedule. Get it in writing first.

My score is bad. Should I bother?

There is no minimum score to check. Most of our lending partners read your deposits first. Your score can still matter to a lender at the end.

I already have an advance. Can I still get funded?

Maybe, but it is harder. Most partners want the one you have mostly paid down first. Say so up front and we will be straight with you.

Am I going to get 20 phone calls?

You will hear from lending partners who have something real to offer. We do not sell your details to a call list.

How fast does this move?

Checking takes about a minute. After that it is up to the lender and how fast you send what they ask for. Nobody can promise you a date.
