WHAT WENT WRONG
---------------
Two faults, both mine.

1. THE SWITCH ALONE DOES NOTHING.
   LeadCapture's own documentation: enabling "Include Form Data in URL"
   opens a FIELD MAPPER, and you then pick which fields go into the URL.
   Nothing is selected by default.

   The API I used only has an on/off flag. There is no parameter for
   choosing the fields. So I flipped the switch and the list behind it
   stayed empty, and your URL arrived carrying nothing. I should have
   checked the documentation before telling you it was wired up.

2. I GUESSED THE PARAMETER NAMES WRONG.
   LeadCapture builds each parameter name from the field LABEL in
   snake_case. "Phone Number" becomes phone_number. I had mapped
   against the funnel's internal field names instead, so even with
   fields selected, most would not have matched.

WHAT I CHANGED
--------------
IN LEADCAPTURE (live already):
  - Redirect moved OFF the "Email" step and ONTO the "Thank You 17"
    step, which is where the builder exposes redirect settings. It was
    on a step whose redirect panel the UI does not show, so you could
    not have found it to fix the field selection.
  - Destination https://lendihub.com/thankyou, 2 second delay,
    UTMs carried, include-field-data ON.

IN THESE FILES:
  The prefill map now matches keys LOOSELY. Case, spaces, hyphens and
  underscores are ignored, and several spellings are listed per field.
  Tested against three different naming conventions -- LeadCapture's
  snake_case default, Ferrylend's own names, and the raw field names --
  and all three produce the identical correct result.

  Practically: you do not need to rename anything in the mapper. Accept
  the defaults and it will work.

### WHAT YOU HAVE TO DO -- THIS IS THE PART THAT UNBLOCKS IT ###

Open the funnel in the LeadCapture builder:

  1. Select the "Thank You 17" step.
  2. Find Redirect After Submission. It is on, pointing at
     https://lendihub.com/thankyou.
  3. Find "Include Form Data in URL". It is on, and underneath it is a
     field mapper listing every field in the funnel.
  4. TICK THESE EIGHT:
         First Name
         Last Name
         Email
         Phone Number
         Business Name
         State
         Loan Amount
         Monthly Business Revenue
     Leave the parameter names at their defaults.
  5. The builder shows a LIVE URL PREVIEW underneath. It should now
     show the parameters. If it is still bare, nothing is ticked.

Do NOT tick time in business, credit score, industry, entity type, use
of funds or outstanding loans. Those are answer bands and the
application wants real values -- that is what produced the empty
TIME IN BUSINESS on your test lead.

THEN TEST
---------
Submit one lead. On the thank-you page open devtools, Console. You will
see one of:

  [Lendihub] forwarded to Ferrylend: ["first_name -> first_name", ...]
      -> the handoff worked.

  [Lendihub] forwarded to Ferrylend: (nothing)
  [Lendihub] not in the map, ignored: [...]
      -> the names arrived but are spelled differently. Send me that
         list and each fix is one line.

  no [Lendihub] line at all
      -> nothing reached the page. The fields are still not ticked.

The address bar will look clean either way -- the page deliberately
strips the personal data out of the URL before your Meta Pixel reads
it. Judge it by the console, not by the address bar.

ONE SIDE EFFECT
---------------
The redirect now sits on the Thank You step with a 2 second delay, so
merchants briefly see LeadCapture's own thank-you screen before landing
on your page. Worth changing that screen's text to something like
"Thanks. Taking you to your application..." so the two seconds read as
intentional.
