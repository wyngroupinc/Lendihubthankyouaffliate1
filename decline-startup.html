How to qualify in a few months

Every funded business started where you are. Here is what our lending partners look for.

🗓️
Get to six months of trading

Time in business is the first gate. Most of our partners want to see at least six months, and a full year opens far more doors. The clock starts when money starts moving through the business, not when you registered the name.

🏦
Run everything through one business account

Open a business checking account now. Run all your income through it. That is the account a lender reads. Money spread across a few accounts makes you look smaller than you are.

📈
Build up to $10,000 a month in deposits

That is the level our main network starts at. Steady months matter more than one big one, so aim for a line that holds rather than a spike. Three to six months at that level puts you in range.

↩️
Come back and try again

There is no penalty for applying more than once. When you hit six months open and $10,000 a month coming in, start over here. It still takes about a minute and it is still free.
