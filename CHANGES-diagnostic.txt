WHY
---
"Didn't work" can mean three completely different problems with three
different fixes, and I have no way to see your browser. This puts the
answer on the screen so neither of us has to guess.

HOW TO USE IT
-------------
Deploy these, then open:

  https://lendihub.com/thankyou?lhdebug=1&first_name=Test&last_name=Merchant&email=test%40example.com&phone=%2B18035551234&business_name=Test%20Company&state=FL&loan_amount=75000&monthly_business_revenue=48000

A dark bar appears at the bottom of the page with the verdict.
Screenshot it and send it to me. No devtools needed.

It only ever shows when ?lhdebug=1 is in the URL. Merchants never see
it. Verified.

WHAT THE THREE ANSWERS MEAN
---------------------------
"HANDOFF OK - 8 field(s) sent to Ferrylend"
    Your side works. If the form above it is still blank, Ferrylend's
    apply page ignores URL parameters. Nothing I can fix in code; it
    becomes an email to them. The "iframe src" line in the panel is the
    proof to send them.

"NOTHING WAS SENT - parameters did arrive, but none matched"
    LeadCapture is naming them something I did not anticipate. The
    "received" list shows the real names. Send it and each fix is one
    line in the map.

"NOTHING WAS SENT - no parameters reached this page at all"
    The eight fields are still not ticked in the LeadCapture field
    mapper, on the Thank You 17 step. That is the fix.

TESTED
------
All three states reproduced locally and the panel gave the right
verdict for each. Confirmed invisible without the flag. No JS errors.
All four inline scripts pass node --check.

A NOTE ON THE LAST ROUND
------------------------
The previous build had a fault I introduced: the debug flag counted
itself as lead data, so a page with no real parameters reported
"parameters did arrive" instead of "none reached this page" - exactly
the wrong diagnosis for the most likely case. Fixed and retested.
