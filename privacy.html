Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, who we give it to, and what you can tell us to stop.

Effective date: September 13, 2026  ·  Last updated: September 13, 2026

The short version

We are a lead generation marketplace. We are not a lender.

When you fill in our form, we send the information you gave us to lending partners who may want to fund you. Those partners pay us for that introduction. Under California law and several other state laws, that counts as selling and sharing your personal information, and we say so plainly below.

If you give us your phone number and tick the consent box, you agree that we and our funding partners may call and text you, including with automated systems.

You can tell us to stop at any time. Your rights and how to use them are in section 8.

1. Who we are and what this covers

Lendihub is operated by Lendihub LLC, a subsidiary of Wyn Group Inc ("Lendihub," "we," "us," "our").

This policy covers lendihub.com and every page and form on it, including our business funding pages and our personal loan pages. It also covers the application forms we host through our form provider, wherever those forms appear.

We are not a lender. We do not approve applications, set rates, decide terms, or lend money. We connect people and businesses with lending partners who do. Once a lending partner receives your information, that partner handles it under its own privacy policy, not this one.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

Depending on which form you fill in, we may collect:

  • About your business: business legal name, business type (LLC, sole proprietor, corporation, partnership, non-profit), industry, the state it is registered in, how long it has been operating, average monthly revenue or deposits into your business account, what you want the funding for, and whether you have other business loans or advances outstanding.
  • About your finances: the amount of funding you are looking for and your own estimate of your credit score range. We ask you to estimate it. We do not pull your credit report to show you options.
  • About you: first name, last name, phone number and email address.
  • Your consent records: the exact consent text shown to you, whether you agreed, and when.

Information we collect automatically

  • IP address, browser type, device type and operating system.
  • Pages you visited, how you arrived, and the date and time.
  • Advertising identifiers, including Meta click and browser identifiers (commonly called fbclid, fbc and fbp) and any campaign tracking parameters in the link you clicked.
  • How far you got through a form, including if you stopped partway.

Information we receive from others

  • Phone verification. When we text you a code to confirm your number, our verification provider tells us whether the code was entered correctly and may return basic information about the line, such as whether it is a mobile or landline number.
  • Advertising platforms. Platforms such as Meta may tell us that a visit came from one of our ads.
  • Lending partners. A partner may tell us whether they were able to fund you, so we know whether an introduction worked.

What we do not collect on this website: we do not ask for your Social Security number, your date of birth, your bank login details, your account or card numbers, or copies of your bank statements or tax returns. A lending partner may ask for some of those later, directly, as part of their own application. That is their process and their privacy policy applies to it.

3. How we collect it

We collect information when you fill in a form on our site, when you browse the site, when you click one of our ads, and when you reply to a call, text or email from us. Our forms are hosted and processed on our behalf by a third party form platform, which stores submissions for us under contract.

4. How we use your information

We use it to:

  • Work out which lending partners are likely to consider your business, and send your information to them.
  • Check whether you meet the basic criteria our partners require, and tell you promptly if you do not.
  • Verify that the phone number you gave us is yours.
  • Contact you about your enquiry by phone, text and email.
  • Answer your questions and provide support.
  • Measure and improve our advertising, including reporting conversions back to advertising platforms.
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse and false submissions.
  • Keep records we are required to keep, including consent records, and comply with the law.

5. How we share your information

We share your information with:

  • Lending partners and funding providers. This is the core of what we do. When you submit a qualifying application, we send the information you gave us to one or more lending partners so they can decide whether to make you an offer. They pay us for this.
  • Lead delivery and CRM platforms. We use third party platforms to route your submission to the right partners and to manage follow-up.
  • Our service providers. Form hosting, phone verification, hosting and infrastructure, email and text messaging, and analytics. They may only use your information to perform services for us.
  • Advertising platforms. We send certain event information to advertising platforms, including Meta, both from your browser and from our servers, so we can measure which ads work. This can include a hashed version of information such as your email address or phone number, along with advertising identifiers.
  • Legal and safety. If we are required to by law, subpoena, or a lawful request, or where we believe it is necessary to protect our rights, our users, or the public.
  • A buyer of our business. If our business is sold, merged, or reorganised, your information may transfer as part of it.

We do not publish your information publicly, and we do not post it to a public directory.

6. Sale and sharing of personal information

We want to be direct about this, because it is how our business works and because the law requires us to disclose it.

We are paid by lending partners when we introduce you to them. Under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act, and under several other state privacy laws, passing your personal information to those partners in exchange for payment is treated as a "sale" of personal information.

Sending advertising event data to platforms such as Meta so they can measure and target advertising is treated as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising.

In the twelve months before the date at the top of this page, we sold or shared the categories of information listed in section 2 for these purposes. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of anyone under 16.

You can opt out. See section 8. Opting out of the sale of your information means we cannot introduce you to lending partners, because that introduction is the sale. You are welcome to opt out; it just means we cannot match you.

We do not sell or share your information for any purpose other than those described here, and we do not sell it to data brokers for unrelated marketing.

7. Calls, texts and emails

If you give us your phone number and tick the consent box on our form, you are giving express written consent for Lendihub and our funding partners to contact you at that number, including by automatic telephone dialling system, prerecorded or artificial voice, and text message, about funding options.

This consent is not a condition of getting information or of any purchase. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies.

To stop text messages, reply STOP to any message. To stop emails, use the unsubscribe link in any email. To stop calls, tell the caller, or contact us using section 15. Withdrawing consent does not affect contact you asked for before you withdrew it, and we may still need to contact you about an application already in progress.

We keep a record of the consent you gave, including the wording shown to you and the time you agreed, because the law requires us to be able to show it.

8. Your privacy rights and how to use them

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we have collected about you, where we got it, why we collected it, and who we disclosed it to.
  • Get a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Delete the personal information we hold about you, subject to exceptions such as records we must keep by law.
  • Correct personal information that is wrong.
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information, including for targeted advertising.
  • Not be discriminated against for using any of these rights. We will not give you a worse service or a different price because you exercised a right.

These rights come from laws including the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended, and comparable laws in states such as Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas, Oregon and Montana. Which rights you have depends on your state.

How to make a request

Email info@lendihub.com with the subject line "Privacy Request." Tell us which right you want to use and give us enough detail to find your record, normally the phone number or email address you used on the form.

To opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information, email the same address with the subject line "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information."

We will confirm we received your request and respond within the time the law allows, normally 45 days, and we will tell you if we need longer. We may need to verify who you are before we act, so that we do not give your information to someone else. An authorised agent may act for you if they provide written permission.

You can also turn on a browser based opt-out preference signal, such as Global Privacy Control. Where the law requires it, we treat that signal as a request to opt out of sale and sharing for that browser.

9. Cookies, pixels and tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to run the site, remember your progress through a form, and measure advertising.

We use the Meta Pixel and the Meta Conversions API. The pixel runs in your browser. The Conversions API sends equivalent information from our servers. Both tell Meta about actions such as viewing a page or submitting a form, so we can measure and target our advertising. Meta handles that information under its own data policy.

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies in their settings. Blocking some cookies may stop parts of the site working. For advertising specifically, you can also use the opt-out tools at optout.aboutads.info and optout.networkadvertising.org, and the ad settings inside your Meta account.

10. How long we keep information

We keep your information for as long as we need it for the purposes in section 4, and for as long as we are required to keep it by law. Consent records for calls and texts are kept for the period required by the rules that govern them. When we no longer need information, we delete it or make it permanently anonymous.

11. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect your information, including encryption of data in transit. No website or method of transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you as required by law.

12. Children

Our services are for adults. We do not direct our site to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Other companies' websites

Our site links to other companies, including lending partners. Once you leave our site, or once a partner has your information, that company's privacy policy governs what happens next. We do not control how they handle your information, and this policy does not apply to them. We suggest reading the privacy policy of any lender before you apply with them.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the "last updated" date at the top. If we make a significant change to how we use or share your information, we will give more prominent notice. Using the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
