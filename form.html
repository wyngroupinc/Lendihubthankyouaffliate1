Important Disclosures: Lendihub is not a lender and does not make credit decisions. We are a free online marketplace that connects business owners with lending partners in our network. Approval, amount, rates, terms and conditions are set entirely by individual lenders under their own criteria. Being matched with a lending partner is not an approval and is not an offer of credit. Not all businesses will qualify, and not all who qualify will receive the full amount requested or the lowest advertised rate.

When you check your options through Lendihub we do not perform any credit inquiry. If you choose to formally apply with a specific lender, that lender may perform a credit inquiry as part of its own process, which could affect your score. Review every rate, fee, payment amount, payment schedule and total repayment amount provided by any lender before accepting an offer.

Lendihub may be compensated by lending partners in its network when a business is matched or completes an application.
