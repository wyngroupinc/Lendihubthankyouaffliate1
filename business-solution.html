Fair questions before you start

Is this one of those predatory things?

You are right to ask. You have heard the stories. Draws that creep up. A payoff number that moves. Nobody picking up when it goes wrong. So here is what we hold every lender to. The payment, how often it comes out, and the total payback all go in writing before you sign.

How many people are going to call me?

We do not blast your number out. Your answers go to lending partners who fund businesses like yours. Not a call list.

Will this hurt my credit?

We do not pull your credit to show you options. A lender may check later, once you decide to go forward with one. That is your call, not ours.

What if I already have an advance?

One is usually fine. If you are paying back two or more, most lenders will want those paid down first. We will tell you that up front instead of wasting your time.

How fast does this move?

You hear from a lender in a few hours during business hours. How fast money moves after that is up to the lender and what they ask you for.

What does this cost me?

Nothing. You never pay us. We get paid by the lender when a match works out.

The No-Surprise Guarantee

Every lender you meet through us puts three things in writing before you sign. The payment. How often it comes out. The total you pay back. You never pay us a thing, and you can stop at any point. Nothing is binding until you sign with a lender yourself.
