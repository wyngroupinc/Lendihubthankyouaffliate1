LENDIHUB — application framed as a dark panel
Built 31 Oct 2026

FILES (2): thankyou.html, thankyou-buyer.html  (identical mirrors)
NO CSS CHANGE. All styling is inline on the new section.

WHY
---
Ferrylend serves that form from their own domain. Cross-origin CSS
cannot reach inside an iframe, so the form cannot be restyled from your
page. I tested the one technique people claim works for this -- setting
color-scheme:light on the iframe element -- in Chromium at both browser
settings. It does nothing. The embedded page follows the viewer's own
system preference regardless of what the parent asks for.

So instead of a dark form sitting in a cream page looking broken, the
section around it is now dark on purpose.

WHAT CHANGED
------------
The application sits in a dark band (#0B1220) with:
  - a green "SECURE APPLICATION" pill with a padlock
  - a white serif heading, matching your Fraunces brand type
  - muted blue-grey supporting copy
  - the iframe in a rounded container with a hairline border and a drop
    shadow, so it reads as a panel resting on the section rather than a
    colour mismatch
  - a small "Encrypted and sent straight to underwriting" line beneath

The slight difference between the band and the form's own black is now
doing work: it separates the panel from its background instead of
looking like a seam.

A DUPLICATION I CAUGHT
----------------------
The hero said "Finish your application below. It takes about five
minutes" and the panel heading underneath said "Finish your
application" with "About five minutes" in its subcopy. The same two
sentences twice, about 200px apart.

Fixed by splitting the jobs:
  hero   -> reassurance. "A funding specialist already has your
            details. The last step takes about five minutes."
  panel  -> the call to action. "Finish your application / Your answers
            carry over, so you only fill in what is missing."

VERIFIED
--------
Rendered at 1280px and 390px with a dark stub in place of the iframe:
no horizontal overflow at either width, no JS errors, panel border and
shadow reading correctly against the band. Screenshot reviewed, not
just diffed.

IF FERRYLEND EVER ADDS A LIGHT THEME
------------------------------------
Delete the two inline background colours on the <section> and the
container div, and drop the text colours back to the normal classes.
The section is marked with a comment explaining why it is dark, so
whoever touches it next knows it was a decision and not an accident.
