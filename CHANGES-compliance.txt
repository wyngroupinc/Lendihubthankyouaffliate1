-----------------------------

PHONE and EMAIL, not SMS. That is what makes the carrier's required
"no sharing" statement truthful without contradicting the fact that you
sell leads, which your privacy policy correctly discloses in sections 5
and 6.

WHY THE OPT-IN FORM SCORED 0/8
------------------------------
Nothing was wrong with your consent wording. The scanner could not SEE
it. lendihub.com/form contains an embed that builds the funnel in
JavaScript, so the HTML a crawler reads is "Loading your application...
Please enable JavaScript to view the form." The hosted funnel URL
(my.leadcapture.io/p/5wwxcpeq) is the same — 276 characters, no form,
no consent text.

The new block in form.html puts the disclosures in readable HTML, which
should carry these six:
  business name · message type · frequency · message & data rates ·
  opt-out instructions · privacy policy & terms links

THE TWO THAT HTML CANNOT FIX
----------------------------
  "Checkbox not pre-checked (explicit consent)"
  "Checkbox is optional (not required)"

Your funnel has NO consent checkbox. The consent block on the Email step
is set to showCheckbox = false, so the wording sits above the button and
clicking the button IS the consent. That is a valid TCPA pattern, but it
means there is no box to inspect, and it also means SMS consent is not
genuinely optional today — you cannot submit without it.

To pass these two you would set, on the Email step consent block:
  showCheckbox      = true
  checkboxRequired  = false

Understand the trade before you do it: leads who do not tick it arrive
WITHOUT SMS consent, and you must not text those people. That is a real
operational change, not a cosmetic one.

I deliberately did NOT write "agreeing to texts is optional" into
form.html, because today it is not.
