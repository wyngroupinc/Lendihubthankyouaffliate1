Before you start

Questions you probably have

How much can I actually get?

It tracks your deposits. A business banking $20,000 a month and one banking $200,000 a month are not looking at the same number. Your statements set it, not your request.

Is this a loan or an advance?

Could be either. It depends on the funding partner and your numbers. You see which one it is, and the terms, before you sign anything.

How fast does money show up?

Many funding partners move in a few business days once they have your statements. Nobody can promise a date before they see the file.

Will this hurt my credit score?

Looking does not. We do not pull your credit. If you go forward with a funding partner, they may check at that point.

What happens to my bank statements?

They go to the funding partners looking at your file. They are not sold and they do not go on any list.

Am I going to get called by ten people?

No. Your file goes to funding partners who fund businesses like yours. It does not go on a call list that gets resold.

Who we are

One file. A network of funding partners.

You send one file. We put it in front of funding partners across the country who fund businesses that look like yours. Not a directory that sells your number to whoever bids the most for it.
