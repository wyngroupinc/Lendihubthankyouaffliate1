Loan approval, terms, rates, and conditions are determined entirely by individual lenders based on their own criteria, including creditworthiness, income, and loan amount requested. Not all consumers will qualify for a loan or the lowest advertised rate.

When you check your options through Lendihub, we do not perform any credit inquiry. We match you with lenders based on the basic information you provide. If you choose to formally apply with a specific lender, that lender may perform a credit inquiry as part of their own process, which could affect your score. Please review all terms provided by any lender before accepting an offer.
