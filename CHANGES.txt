Drop all files into the repo root, replacing what is there.

NEW PAGES
  working-capital.html   ~1,000 words
  term-loans.html        ~900 words
  line-of-credit.html    ~870 words

  Each one: hero + CTA, what it is, how repayment works, who it fits
  and who it does not (honest both ways), the cost framing owners get
  wrong, a three-way comparison block with the current page marked,
  six FAQs, final CTA. All CTAs go to form.html — one funnel, as before.

  Canonical tags set. robots index,follow. Reading level 4.0–4.4,
  inside your 5th-grade rule.

  NO amounts, rates, factor rates, approval times or term lengths
  anywhere. Nothing to defend later, nothing to update when the
  funder panel changes.

WORKING CAPITAL covers merchant cash advance / revenue-based funding
  honestly, including how daily and weekly remittance actually works
  and when it goes badly. "Ask this before you sign" box included.

TERM LOANS carries the credit-qualifier positioning: a plain
  "what helps you / what gets in the way" list naming credit, time
  in business, deposit stability and existing advances. Someone who
  does not fit learns it in a minute instead of three weeks.

NAVIGATION
  Desktop: logo | Working Capital · Term Loans · Line of Credit | Get Funded
  Mobile:  logo | Get Funded
  The product links are hidden below 900px on purpose. Four links plus
  the logo crushes the mark at 390px — that is the bug we hit in
  September. On mobile the products are reachable from the homepage
  cards instead, which converts better than a cramped nav anyway.

HOMEPAGE
  New "Funding options" card section sits between the hero and the
  gap section, on index.html and business.html. Three cards, one per
  product. Stacks to one column below 760px.

CSS
  New block at the end of BOTH styles.css and vertical.css:
  .nav__product, .product-grid, .product-card and friends.
  It is in both files on purpose — vertical.css loads after styles.css
  at equal specificity, so a rule in only one of them gets silently
  overridden. That caught us once already.

VERIFIED
  All 12 pages: div / section / footer / nav tags balanced, every
  internal link resolves. Rendered at 390px and 1280px — no horizontal
  scroll, no JS errors, one footer per page. Nav confirmed to show
  four links at 1280 and one at 390.

NOTE ON TWO BUGS I CAUGHT

  it. Same thing happened earlier with the partners page edit. If you

  not just diff.
