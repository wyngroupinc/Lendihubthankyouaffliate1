Application Update

Nonprofits get funded. Just not by us.

Our partners lend to businesses that sell things. They get paid back out of those sales. A nonprofit runs on donations and grants. That is the whole reason, and it is not about your size or your work.

Why wasn't I matched?

Our lenders read your business bank account. They look at the money that comes in from sales each month. Then they take their payment back out of it. A nonprofit does not work that way. Your money comes from donors and grants. Lenders also ask an owner to sign for the loan. A nonprofit has no owner to sign. Those two things stop the match every time.

Four Open Doors

Where the money comes from instead

These four doors are open to you. Our lending partners are not one of them.

🏛️
Loan funds for nonprofits

These lenders only work with nonprofits. Some charge low interest. A few charge none at all. Look up Nonprofit Finance Fund, Propel Nonprofits, and Open Road Ventures. Most want to see that you have been running for a while.

🤝
Local lenders

A CDFI is a community lender. They lend where big banks will not. Loans are often fifty thousand dollars or less. The paperwork takes longer. They also say yes more often.

🏦
Credit unions and small banks

Big banks rarely lend to nonprofits. Credit unions and small local banks do it much more. Start with the one that holds your account. They can already see your money.

🎁
Grants

Grant money is free. You never pay it back. Federal and state grants are listed at grants.gov. Many large companies give money too. The forms take work. The money is yours to keep.

A no from us is not a no from everyone.

Nonprofits get money every day. They just go through other doors.
